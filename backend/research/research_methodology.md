# Research methodology

The research question is whether NLP classification, duplicate retrieval, resolution estimation, and event-log analysis can make student-service handling more understandable and timely. Establish simple baselines before evaluating improved methods. Freeze data versions and seeds, preserve train/test separation, document labels and missingness, and report macro category metrics as well as per-class errors.

The included synthetic data verifies a runnable pipeline only. It does not support a claim that CAMPUSINTEL improves categorization, routing, delay prediction, or institutional performance. A college pilot needs approved de-identified historical requests, reviewed labels, event histories with reliable timestamps, and a prospective shadow evaluation. Keep model suggestions visible as unverified and preserve human control of routing and outcomes.
